# Prescription refill procedure

Fictional, modeled on common retail pharmacy practice. Not legal or clinical guidance.

## Refills

A 30-day supply may be refilled once 80% of it has elapsed, counted from the fill date in the pharmacy record.

A refill requires at least 1 refill remaining on the prescription.

Schedule II controlled substances cannot be refilled; the prescriber must issue a new prescription.

Nothing is dispensed until the patient's identity has been verified.

When the refill is due, a refill remains, the medicine is not Schedule II and the patient's identity is verified, the pharmacist dispenses the refill.

## When a refill cannot be dispensed

If the patient's identity has not been verified, verify it before anything else.

When the patient has no refills left or the medicine is Schedule II, contact the prescriber for a new prescription.

When the patient is early, tell them the day the refill becomes available.

## Emergency supply

When a patient is due and has no refills left, the prescriber cannot be reached, and the medicine is not Schedule II, the pharmacist dispenses an emergency supply of 3 days.

## Notices

Every request gets an outcome notice.

Staff should be courteous and thank the patient for choosing the pharmacy.
